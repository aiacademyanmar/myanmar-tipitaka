## Editorial and legal safeguards
- Pāli source, translation and explanation are separate data classes.
- AI drafts are never marked approved by the build.
- Reviewed Myanmar import requires two distinct reviewers, rights evidence, source reference, review reference/date and exact Pāli SHA-256.
- Bilara Pāli source is not automatically equated to the MCU/Thai Tipitaka.cloud 45-volume edition boundaries.
- Source licence and redistribution rights must be verified before shipping the data in APK.

## QA and release policy
- Compile smoke test may pass while source corpus is incomplete.
- APK artifact upload remains blocked until SQLite integrity, all 45 volume mappings and content release gates pass.
- No claim of 45-book offline availability or complete Myanmar coverage until verified counts and artifact QA are available.
