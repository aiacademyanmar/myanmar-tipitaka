## Critical data-model distinction
The acquired `pali_root_source.sqlite` is a source staging database with `source_segments` and `build_info`. It is NOT the Android reader database and is NOT copied into `app/src/main/assets/tipitaka_offline.sqlite`.

The Android reader's current installer expects a different schema:
- paragraphs(id, volume, body, source_ref)
- paragraph_explanations(...)

The source staging database must first pass an editorial crosswalk and then be transformed into the exact native-reader schema. Never point the current installer at the staging database.

## Edition mapping limitation
SuttaCentral/Bilara source families are candidates for mapping. Their presence, file names or lexicographic endpoints do not prove the 45 printed MCU volume boundaries. Current crosswalk status remains unverified until actual edition-specific start/end references and reviewer evidence are supplied.

The acquisition workflow intentionally creates a research artifact, not a complete 45-book APK corpus.
