## Implemented
1. Added MyanmarEditorialActivity, a native on-device workbench for selecting an existing Pali paragraph by volume and paragraph ID.
2. Loads the original Pali paragraph from the installed offline corpus in read-only mode.
3. Provides separate Myanmar translation and Myanmar explanation draft fields.
4. Captures source reference, rights/reuse evidence, two reviewer names and review reference.
5. Saves drafts to a separate private SQLite database (myanmar_editorial_drafts.db), not to the shipped Pali corpus.
6. Supports Draft and Submitted for Review states. Submission requires two distinct reviewer names plus source, rights and review evidence fields.
7. Adds a draft list and reload function, keyed by volume + paragraph ID.
8. Adds the workbench to the Tipitaka contents menu and registers it in AndroidManifest.
9. Updates Android version metadata and CI workflow to 2.3.4.9.8 / 41.

## Safety and editorial integrity
- This UI does not claim that typed reviewer names prove that review actually occurred.
- It has no approve, reject, or publish action.
- It does not write draft content into the read-only Pali corpus or make it appear as approved content in the reader.
- A future controlled export/import process must verify real reviewer evidence, reuse rights, source references and exact Pali SHA-256 before publication.
- Draft storage is local to this app installation; it is not cloud-synced or backed up by this feature.

## QA limitations
The GitHub workflow checks the workbench source is present and compiles the Android app. Corpus/crosswalk gates remain independent. A debug compile does not mean the full 45-book corpus or Myanmar translations/explanations are complete.
